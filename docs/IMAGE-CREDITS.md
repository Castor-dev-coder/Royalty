The 16 `qa-defense-assets/` rows (appended 2026-10-27) are **illustrative QA demo
images** for the QA-defence demo — placeholder team-profile portraits and
planned sample-review photos only; they are not real client content and not
live UI assets. Files live in `/home/jeremy/qa-defense-assets/` (not in the
frontend bundle): each was downloaded from the Pexels CDN with HTTP 200 and
processed to a valid JPEG ≥20KB, max 1280px long edge, quality ~80, EXIF
stripped. The 14 review shots cover hair color, nails, haircuts, styling, and
salon details (no payment screens, receipts, or QR imagery). Grouping for the
8 planned QA sample reviews (3, 3, 2, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1 photos) is recorded in
`qa-defense-assets/manifest.json`.

Category names match the seeded `ServiceCategory` rows in
`backend/scripts/seed.ts` (`Hair`, `Nails`, `Hair Cutting`, `Hair Coloring`).

  (`public/images/empty-states/`).

  under `public/images/empty-states/`.
- **Reviews** — review photos are real uploads from the `salon-media` bucket;
  not replaced or fabricated.
- **GCash QR** — none uploaded in payment settings; none fabricated here.
